# Testing the site with applicants

Run this with **5 applicants** who have not seen the site. Use their own phones.
Takes about 10 minutes each.

## Setup

1. Send them the link: https://aipprgm.github.io
2. Say: "I'm testing the website, not you. Please think out loud. I can't help you
   until you're done."
3. Time each task from when you finish reading it to when they find the answer.

## Tasks

Read each task out loud, one at a time.

| # | Task | Success means |
|---|------|---------------|
| 1 | "Your employer changed your job title on 1 October. Find the last day you can send IRCC your new documents." | They state the correct date from the deadline tool. |
| 2 | "Open the C18 restoration, reconsideration and SOWP tracker." | The Google Sheet opens. |
| 3 | "Join the WhatsApp group." | The WhatsApp join screen opens. |

## Record

For each person and task: **done / not done**, **seconds**, and **where they
tapped first**.

## Pass mark

At least **4 of 5** people complete each task in **under 60 seconds**.
If a task fails, note where people tapped first; that is where they expected
to find it.

## Afterwards

Watch the WhatsApp group for a month. Fewer repeat questions about deadlines,
trackers and timelines mean the site is doing its job.

## Automated checks

Run Lighthouse in Chrome (DevTools → Lighthouse → Mobile). Target 90+ for
Accessibility and Performance.
