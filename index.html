    What do the abbreviations mean?
    AOR
    Acknowledgement of Receipt: IRCC has your application.
    PAL
    Pre-Arrival Letter: invites you to free settlement services.
    ADR
    Additional Document Request: IRCC asks for more or updated documents.
    FD
    Final decision on your PR application.
    PPR
    Passport request, for applicants outside Canada.
    COPR / eCOPR
    (Electronic) Confirmation of Permanent Residence.
    C18
    The code for the AIP employer-specific work permit.
    WPSL
    Work Permit Support Letter (referral letter) from the province.
    SOWP
    Spousal Open Work Permit.
    BOWP
    Bridging Open Work Permit. Not available to AIP applicants.
    ECA
    Educational Credential Assessment, for education outside Canada.
    CLB / TEER
    Canadian Language Benchmark level / job skill level in the National Occupational Classification (NOC).

          C18 work permit checklist

          The C18 is an employer-specific work permit, valid for up to 2 years, that lets you work for your designated employer while your PR application is processed. Apply online only; you can't apply at the border. Most members apply for PR first. IRCC only requires it if your endorsement certificate has expired: you must have applied for PR before it expired. AIP applicants can't get a bridging open work permit (BOWP).

          Language test: must be less than 2 years old on the day you apply

          This applies to your first C18, unless you already submitted your PR application with valid results. You need at least CLB 5 for TEER 0–3 jobs, or CLB 4 for TEER 4, in all four skills. Renewals with the same employer don't need language results.

          1. Before you apply: your employer's part

          1. Provincial endorsement and Work Permit Support Letter (WPSL)The WPSL, also called the referral letter, comes from the province. Your employer requests it when applying for endorsement.
          2. Offer of employment numberYour employer submits the offer in the IRCC Employer Portal, sets the duration to 2 years and pays the $230 compliance fee. The number usually starts with “A”. You need it to apply, and it can't be reused.

          2. First application: what to upload

          1. One file named “Client information” (4 MB max) containing:your WPSL; your signed IMM 0156 undertaking; your education credentials (plus an ECA under 5 years old if you studied outside Canada); your language test results; and proof of work experience (letters of employment showing at least 1 year full-time, or 1,560 hours, in the last 5 years). International graduates send proof of 16 months living in the province within the 24 months before graduating, and of valid status, instead of work experience.
          2. The offer of employment number from your employerYou enter it in the application form.
          3. Forms and checklist the portal gives youIMM 5710 and checklist IMM 0163 if you're inside Canada; IMM 1295 and checklist IMM 0162 if you're outside.
          4. Passport, digital photo and current permitYour permit can't run past your passport's expiry date, so renew your passport first if it expires within 2 years.
          5. Fees$155 for the work permit, plus $85 for biometrics if IRCC asks for them.

          Not on IRCC's C18 list: the endorsement certificate, the Offer of Employment form (IMM 0157, which is for your PR application), a settlement plan or pay stubs. Add them only if IRCC asks.

          3. Renewing with the same employer

          1. A new offer of employment numberYour employer submits a new offer and pays the $230 compliance fee again.
          2. Proof you applied for PR, if your endorsement has expiredYour AOR, showing you applied before the endorsement certificate expired.
          3. Not needed:WPSL, IMM 0156, language results, education credentials or work experience proof. If you change employer, send an updated WPSL and a new IMM 0156.

          From the group: one refusal mentioned the province's continued support, and some members asked the province for an updated WPSL to be safe. A missing IMM 0156 has also caused refusals of first applications.

          Sources: IRCC, exemption code C18 · IRCC, AIP temporary work permit (spouses can apply for an open work permit) · Last checked 17 Nov 2026

          What's usually in an ADR

          An Additional Document Request (ADR) is IRCC asking, through your portal, for updated or missing documents. Not everyone gets one, and it can arrive before or after your PAL. This list comes from what members of the group report being asked for, most common first. Your letter is what counts: send exactly what it lists, by its deadline.

          1. Updated Schedule A (Background/Declaration, IMM 5669)Asked of almost everyone. Check every date and address against your original; explain any corrections.
          2. Employment reference letterOn company letterhead with your duties, hours, wage and start date. The NOC and duties should match your application.
          3. Recent pay stubsOften the last 6 months (about 12–13 stubs). Keep your hours full-time; long unpaid leave shows up here.
          4. Employment records and tax documentsSuch as T4s or notices of assessment, when IRCC wants to confirm your work history.
          5. Military service details (IMM 5546)If you or a family member did military or police service.
          6. Offer of Employment (IMM 0157) or updated endorsementEspecially if anything about your job has changed. See job changes.
          7. Family Information form (IMM 5406)For you or your spouse, sometimes asked again after PAL.
          8. Police certificates or a medical requestLess common in the ADR itself; a medical request often arrives around the same time.
          9. Education documentsDegree certificates, transcripts, and your ECA.
          10. Civil documents for family membersMarriage or birth certificates when you add or include dependants.
          11. Letter of explanationFor gaps or dates that don't line up across your forms.

          Sources: reports from the AIP applicants group (2024–2026) and public forums, checked against IRCC's AIP examination instructions. Not an official IRCC list.

          Your job or employer changed

          If your employer, job role or employer ownership changes during your PR application, IRCC now asks you to send an amended endorsement and other documents within 90 days. If you don't, your application will be refused. Before this change, files were put on hold.

          Source: IRCC, AIP: other situations · Shared in the group, Nov 2026
